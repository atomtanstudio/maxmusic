# MaxMusic — 15-second launch promo

- Product: MaxMusic, a local-first studio for MiniMax Music 3
- Audience: musicians, producers, and local-AI builders who want ownership of their songs and files
- Goal: show the complete path from an idea to a structured song, a durable visual library, and a kinetic lyric video
- Format: 15 seconds, 16:9, landscape
- Visual system: near-black interface, cyan/magenta/orange waveform light, crisp real product UI, cinematic depth without inventing features
- Soundtrack: the first 15 seconds of the bundled MaxMusic sample “Neon Mile”
- CTA: land on the MaxMusic identity; repository copy carries “Make songs on your own computer.”

## Verified product facts used

1. Simple creation accepts a one-line song idea and renders audio.
2. Studio exposes lyrics, structured section tags, arrangement direction, duration, takes, seed, and output format.
3. The library keeps songs and artwork and supports playback and download.
4. Vocal songs can be turned into lyric videos; the captured “Neon Mile” output uses kinetic typography over a directed visual treatment.
